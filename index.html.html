02 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Useful things are
being left behind.

A product can still have value even when its owner no longer needs it. Without a simple way to repair, resell or recycle it, that value can be lost.

The everyday problem

01

Too much replacement
People may replace products that could be repaired.

02

Lost affordability
Buying brand-new products isn't always budget-friendly.

03

Waste to manage
Discarded electronics need safe, specialised handling.

THE BUSINESS GAP

Make the better choice the easier choice.

ReLoop brings collection, checking, repair and resale together in one simple service.

Our opportunity
Customers save money, useful products stay in use longer, and specialist recyclers handle what can't be saved.
THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE02 / 10
03 / THE SOLUTION

One loop. More value.

ReLoop is a local-first service for items that still have life left in them — starting with small electronics and school essentials.

Repair what works

Qualified partner technicians check and repair suitable items before resale.

Reuse with confidence

Clean, check and clearly label second-hand products so buyers know what they're getting.

Recycle responsibly

Route unsalvageable electronics to authorised e-waste recycling partners.

✨ Our promise: affordable products, clear condition labels and responsible handling — never unsafe DIY dismantling of e-waste.Who we serve: students and families · budget-conscious buyers · small offices and schools.
